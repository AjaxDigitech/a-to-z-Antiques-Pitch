Our Story

More Than a Shop—
A Living Museum

Nestled in the heart of Cottondale, A to Z Antiques has been a cornerstone of the local community for generations. Founded with a simple belief—that every object carries a story worth preserving—we've spent decades sourcing extraordinary pieces from estate sales, private collections, and far-flung auctions.

When you walk through our doors, you're not just shopping. You're walking through history. Our knowledgeable staff is always on hand to share the provenance of each item and help you find exactly what you're looking for.
